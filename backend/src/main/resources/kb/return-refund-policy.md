# ShopEase Return & Refund Policy

## Return window
Customers may return most items within **30 days of the delivery date**. The delivery date shown in the order details is the start of the return window. Items delivered more than 30 days ago are not eligible for return or refund, except where required by applicable law.

## Eligibility conditions
- The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and with all tags and accessories included.
- Damaged or defective items on arrival are eligible for a full refund or free replacement regardless of the 30-day window, provided the damage is reported within 7 days of delivery.
- Digital gift cards and downloadable products are non-refundable.

## Refund method and timeline
- Approved refunds are credited to the **original payment method** within **5–7 business days** after approval.
- For Cash-on-Delivery orders, refunds are issued via bank transfer after the customer shares bank details with support.
- Shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to a ShopEase error (wrong item, damaged item, or defective product).

## Refund approval process
Refunds are reviewed and approved by a human support agent. The AI support assistant can recommend a refund, but only a human agent can approve it. Customers are notified in the chat once a decision is made.

## How to request a return or refund
Ask the ResolveAI assistant in the support chat with your order number (for example SE-1003). The assistant verifies eligibility and creates a refund recommendation for human review. You can also ask to speak to a human agent at any time.
