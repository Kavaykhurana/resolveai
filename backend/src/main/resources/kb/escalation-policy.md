# ShopEase Escalation & Human Support Policy

## When to escalate to a human agent
The ResolveAI assistant transfers the conversation to a human support agent when:
- The customer explicitly asks for a human.
- The issue is outside standard policies (legal threats, safety issues, press inquiries).
- The assistant cannot resolve the issue confidently after reasonable attempts.
- The customer is distressed or reports a serious problem such as a damaged high-value item.

## What happens after escalation
- The conversation is marked ESCALATED and a HIGH priority ticket is created with a summary.
- A human agent reviews the full chat transcript and the assistant's action history, then replies **in the same chat**.
- Human support hours: 9 AM – 9 PM IST, all days. Typical first response after escalation: under 2 hours.

## Refund decisions
Only human agents approve or reject refund recommendations created by the assistant. Customers see the decision as a message in the chat.

## Contact channels
Support chat (this assistant) is the primary channel. Email support@shopease.example is answered within 24 hours. There is no phone support.
