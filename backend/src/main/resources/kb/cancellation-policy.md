# ShopEase Cancellation Policy

## Before shipping
Orders in **PROCESSING** status can be cancelled free of charge at any time. Ask the ResolveAI assistant to create a cancellation ticket with the order number; prepaid amounts are refunded automatically within 5–7 business days.

## After shipping
Orders already **SHIPPED** cannot be cancelled in transit. Customers may refuse delivery — the package returns to ShopEase and the refund is issued after it reaches the warehouse (usually 7–10 business days). Alternatively, accept delivery and use the 30-day return window.

## Delayed orders
If an order is **DELAYED** beyond 10 days of the original delivery estimate, customers may cancel for a full refund regardless of shipping status.

## Cancelled orders
A **CANCELLED** order needs no further action; any prepaid refund is already in progress.
