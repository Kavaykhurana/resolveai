# ShopEase Payment & Billing FAQ

## Accepted payment methods
UPI, credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking, popular wallets, and Cash on Delivery (COD, available for orders under Rs.5,000).

## When am I charged?
Prepaid orders are charged at checkout. COD orders are paid in cash or via UPI to the delivery partner at delivery.

## Failed or double charges
If money was deducted but the order shows failed, the amount is auto-reversed within 5–7 business days. For duplicate charges, ask the ResolveAI assistant to create a billing ticket with the order number; the billing team reconciles within 48 hours.

## Invoices
A GST invoice is emailed after delivery. Customers can request a duplicate invoice from support with the order number.

## Refund credits
Approved refunds go to the original payment method (see Return & Refund Policy). COD refunds require bank details and are processed by bank transfer within 5–7 business days after approval.
