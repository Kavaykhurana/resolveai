# ShopEase Warranty FAQ

## What warranty do products carry?
All electronics sold on ShopEase carry a **1-year manufacturer warranty** from the delivery date, covering manufacturing defects. Accessories (cases, pads, stands) carry a 6-month ShopEase replacement warranty.

## What is not covered?
Physical damage, water damage, normal wear and tear, and damage from misuse are not covered. Warranty claims require the order number as proof of purchase — no separate registration is needed.

## How do I claim warranty?
1. Ask the ResolveAI assistant to create a warranty ticket with your order number and a description of the defect.
2. The support team arranges a free pickup for inspection within 3–5 business days.
3. If the defect is confirmed, you receive a repair, replacement, or refund (at ShopEase's discretion) within 10 business days.

## Warranty vs return window
The 30-day return window (see Return & Refund Policy) is for no-questions-asked returns of unused items. Warranty claims apply to defects discovered after the return window, up to 1 year from delivery.
