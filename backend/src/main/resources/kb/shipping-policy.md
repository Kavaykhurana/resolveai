# ShopEase Shipping Policy

## Delivery timelines
- **Standard shipping**: 3–7 business days across India, free for orders above Rs.499.
- **Express shipping**: 1–2 business days in metro cities, Rs.99 per order.
- Orders are processed within 24 hours of being placed (status PROCESSING), then handed to the courier (status SHIPPED).

## Order statuses explained
- **PROCESSING**: order confirmed, being packed at the warehouse.
- **SHIPPED**: handed to the courier; a tracking id (format TRK-xxxxx-IN) is available in order details.
- **DELIVERED**: package delivered; the delivery date starts the 30-day return window.
- **DELAYED**: the courier reported a delay; the revised estimate is usually 2–4 extra days. If a delayed order does not arrive within 10 days of the original estimate, customers are entitled to a full refund or free re-shipment.
- **CANCELLED**: order cancelled; any prepaid amount is refunded automatically within 5–7 business days.

## Tracking
Customers can ask the ResolveAI assistant "Where is my order SE-xxxx?" to get the latest status and tracking id. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear on the courier website after shipping.

## Lost packages
If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, contact support. ShopEase will open a courier investigation and offer a replacement or full refund if the package is confirmed lost.
